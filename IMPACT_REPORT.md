# Impact Framework

This describes the *intended* impact mechanisms. Figures in the prototype are illustrative; no real-world outcomes are claimed.

## Theory of change
Fragmented data → unified cross-domain platform → faster, better-informed decisions by agencies, cities, companies, individuals and NGOs → reduced disaster impact, lower emissions, better-protected ecosystems.

## Impact areas
- **Climate resilience** — earlier warning supports evacuation and pre-positioning of resources.
- **Public health** — air-quality alerts for vulnerable groups.
- **Water security** — proactive rationing and infrastructure planning.
- **Biodiversity** — prioritizing scarce conservation funding.
- **Carbon accountability** — personal and company footprint with reduction actions.
- **Civic engagement** — connecting volunteers with organizations; climate literacy.

## UN SDG alignment
SDG 3 (Air Quality) · SDG 6 (Water) · SDG 7 (Energy) · SDG 11 (Smart Cities) · SDG 13 (Climate Action) · SDG 14 (Ocean) · SDG 15 (Forests/Biodiversity)

## How to measure real impact after launch
1. Change in alert-to-response time in partner jurisdictions vs. baseline.
2. Independently verified emissions reductions from customer action plans.
3. NGO-reported volunteer hours and completed initiatives.
4. Pre/post assessment results for Learning Hub completers.

Do not publish impact numbers until these are measured.
